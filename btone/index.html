Where search can grow

Beyond the fixes, three additions would give btonefitness.com more to rank for, and more for AI engines to cite. The site is already one of the most-cited domains in AI answers about its category, so fresh, specific content has a real audience.

Location search for repeat visitors

A store-finder that detects or remembers a visitor’s nearest studio and sends them straight to that location’s page.

Why it matters: btone has studios across Boston’s neighborhoods. Localizing the experience gets returning visitors to schedule and pricing in one click and strengthens engagement on the location pages that need it most.

FAQs on location and class pages

Short, direct answers to what new clients ask: what to wear, what a reformer class is like, how pricing and intro offers work.

Why it matters: AI engines are already fielding questions like “best pilates studios near me for beginners.” Clear FAQs give them accurate answers to cite from btone’s own site, and they unlock FAQ schema.

A blog with fresh content

A steady publishing cadence on Pilates for beginners, reformer vs. mat, studio news by neighborhood, and seasonal Boston topics.

Why it matters: Fresh pages widen the set of searches btone can show up for and give AI engines recent, specific material to draw from instead of leaning on aggregator listings.
