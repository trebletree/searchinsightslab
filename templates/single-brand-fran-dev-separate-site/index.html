How AI describes [Client]
  • A home improvement franchise built on proprietary, weather-adjusted coatings that give a factory finish.
  • Two revenue streams from one brand: exterior painting and kitchen cabinet refinishing.
  • Home-based with no storefront, and an owner-operator model, not a passive investment.
  • A higher investment than most painting franchises, with a 7% royalty.
Where the answers disagree
  • Investment: six different ranges in one day. $191,015 to $255,415 is the most common, and others start as low as $132,500.
  • Net worth required: $250,000 in most answers, $200,000 in others.
  • Franchise fee: $54,500 in most answers, $49,500 in two.
  • What [Client] is: Claude described it as an automotive paint repair franchise in one answer, and as a cabinet and furniture refinishing franchise in another.
Worth watching
  • A Reddit thread and a Glassdoor review were used as sources, and one platform summarized both when asked about owner reviews.
  • Several answers say owner turnover has run higher than is typical for home services franchises.
  • Some answers say the model needs about $500,000 a year in sales before it pays the owner well.
  • Two platforms said there is little independent owner feedback to go on. Franchise Business Review’s Culture100 recognition appeared in 2 of the 54 answers.

AI answers change from day to day. These come from one day and show the kinds of answers a buyer may see.

Top opportunities

What would help AI assistants recommend [Client], and describe it accurately.

Give AI one set of numbers to quote

One page with the current investment range, franchise fee, royalty and financial requirements, dated with the FDD year. Then update the franchise directory profiles that still show older figures, so AI quotes [Client] and not an outdated listing.

Answer the cost, earnings and financing questions

[Client] is missing from all 42 answers on these topics. It offers a veteran discount, and none of the six answers about financing and veteran discounts named it. A page for each topic gives AI something to cite.

Put more owner voices where AI looks

AI looks for what owners say and finds a few forum posts. Named owner stories with markets and opening dates, and the Franchise Business Review results, give it more to draw on.

What would widen the search footprint.

Move the page-two rankings onto page one

Build pages for markets and for cabinet refinishing

Website

A quick audit of the 87 indexable pages at [clientfranchisesite.com]: the five findings that matter most for Google and AI, and how to get the most from a separate franchise site.

Top 5 findings from a quick audit

This is a quick look, not the full audit. A full technical audit scores every item and lays out every fix.

Top priority

The Investment page does not state the total investment

Issue
The page lists each cost line and the Item 19 highlights. The total range ($191,015 to $255,415), the royalty and the net worth requirement are not on it. The total appears on the Reviews page, and the FAQ says $200,000 net worth where directory listings say $250,000. AI assistants filled the gap with six different ranges.
Fix
Put the total range, franchise fee, royalty and financial requirements on the Investment page, with the FDD year. Have the FAQ and every other mention match it.
Top priority

Retired pages and missing addresses all lead to the homepage

Issue
25 retired pages redirect to the homepage, including the old cost and FAQ pages. 32 redirecting addresses are still linked inside the site, and one is in the main menu on every page. The sitemap address in robots.txt also lands on the homepage, and so does any address that does not exist.
Fix
Point each retired page to its closest current page, update the internal links, correct the sitemap address in robots.txt, and show a not-found page for addresses that do not exist.
Structural

Schema covers the basics only

Issue
The eight pages we checked carry page, breadcrumb and website markup. There is no organization markup, no FAQ markup on a 4,600-word FAQ, and no article markup on the blog.
Fix
Add organization markup that ties the franchise site to [clientsite.com], FAQ markup on the FAQ, and article markup on posts. Schema gets validated in the full audit.
Structural

Titles and descriptions run past what Google shows

Issue
57 of 87 titles are over 60 characters, often because the [Client] name is added at the end, so Google cuts them off. 40 descriptions are over 160 characters and 12 pages have none.
Fix
Lead each title with its topic and drop the brand name, since Google already shows the site name. Trim the long descriptions and write the 12 that are missing.
Working well

The pages buyers look for are in place

Issue
Every indexable page has a unique title and 81 of 87 say franchise. The site has an investment page, a long FAQ, owner stories and a reviews page, and [clientsite.com] links to it from its header and footer.
Fix
Keep the structure. The work is to tighten what is there, not to rebuild.

Getting the most from a separate franchise site

[Client] already has the setup we usually recommend: a franchise site that is separate from the consumer site. A user experience update first, then four practices that help it stand on its own.

Make it clear how to hop back to the consumer site

[clientsite.com] links to the franchise site from its header and footer, which is right. The franchise homepage has no link back, and homeowners searching “[client name, spelled one way]” land on it. Add a clearly labeled link in the header, such as “Looking for painting services?”.

Earn links to the franchise domain itself

A separate domain does not inherit the consumer site’s strength: the franchise domain’s authority score is 16, half of the consumer site’s 32. The Franchise Business Review awards, press coverage and directory profiles are natural places to ask for a link to [clientfranchisesite.com]. Link building and guest posts on franchise and business publications add to that steadily, and are services we offer.

Tell Google and AI the two sites are one brand

Neither search engines nor AI assistants are told the two domains belong together. Organization markup on both sites that names the other connects them, and helps AI describe [Client] as a painting and coatings brand.

Send each search to the right audience

The franchise site ranks second for “[client name, spelled one way]”, a homeowner search. Its titles already say franchise, which helps. A service link at the top of the franchise site gives those visitors a way through.

Measure the franchise site on its own

Give the franchise site its own Search Console property and a working sitemap address, so franchise leads and franchise searches are reported apart from homeowner traffic.
